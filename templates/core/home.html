A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Nice to know.

A little clarity before you get started.

Is ToolSathi really free?

Yes. Every published tool is free to use, including processing, copying, and downloading results. Reasonable upload and request limits protect the service for everyone.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Accounts are optional and only add conveniences such as syncing favorites. All core tools work without signing in.

What happens to my files and data?

Supported text tools process data directly in your browser. Tools that require Python temporarily process your uploads on the server, remove input files after processing, and expire downloadable results. We never save tool contents in your favorites or history. Read our privacy page for details.

Can I use ToolSathi on my phone?

Yes. Every tool adapts to your screen. You can also install the progressive web app on supported browsers. Visit the app page for Android availability and installation instructions.

Can I download the results?

Yes. Each working tool offers a suitable file download. Text results can also be copied directly to your clipboard.
